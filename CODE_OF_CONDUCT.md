# Code of conduct

AlbertCode's issues, discussions and pull requests are places to build software together. Everyone
taking part is expected to help keep them that way.

**Expected**

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including when you disagree.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Help newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- Stay on topic, and keep your reports specific enough to act on.

**Not accepted**

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery, in any form.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, including vote-for-vote or star-for-star requests.

**Enforcement**

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people who
repeatedly or seriously break them.

Report a problem privately through the repository's **Security** tab
(*Report a vulnerability*, with a title starting "Conduct:"). Reports are read only by the
maintainers, and are handled confidentially.
